# Ink Duel Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Deliver a playable canvas duel against a local opponent with destructible ink, composable signs, and spectacular but readable effects.

**Architecture:** Separate game entry point and deterministic fixed-step simulation. Reuse the existing symbol catalogue and spell grammar; translate supported operations into game mechanics explicitly. Render a top-down Three.js scene with Rapier collisions, using dependencies already vendored by the simulator.

**Tech Stack:** Plain HTML/CSS/ES modules, Three.js, Rapier, node:test, browser QA.

**Spec:** Approved gameplay in this conversation: defend an inkwell, localized destructible strokes, matter sigils plus modifier signs, standard and experimental rules. Initial release is against a local AI, not network multiplayer.

## Scope and invariants

- Preserve the editor, public saved canvases, Circle Commons, and local app development.
- Work from a fresh public-main worktree after fetching current remote state. Do not ship unrelated changes from this historical checkout.
- Every one of the 29 sigils and 40 signs must have an explicit supported, experimental, or excluded policy. Unsupported entries cannot silently become generic damaging attacks.
- A plain ink line is not a solid wall. It is destructible magical notation. Physical protection comes from compatible effects and world objects.
- Orientation, positioning, target compatibility, resource availability, and broken rings affect execution.
- No database, authentication, or multiplayer infrastructure changes.

## Initial tuning

30 s preparation, 300 s combat, at most 60 s overtime when tied. Destroyed inkwell ends the match; compare remaining health at final timeout, allow a draw. Arena 24 x 14, inkwells 18 units apart, 300 health each. Start with 100 ink, cap 150, regenerate 3/s. Drawing costs 2 ink per world-unit times thickness. Standard local stroke segment: 100 health; fire 5 damage/s, inkwell fire 10/s. Repair 8 health/s for 4 ink/s, interrupted by damage. Activation windup 1 s, three active seals per player. Upkeep tiers 1/2/4 ink/s, base range 8, projectile speed 4. Stop lasts at most 1.5 s with 8 s immunity. Three personal quick compositions, priced identically to freehand.

These numbers are testable defaults, not claims of canonical behavior. Inkwells and ink resources cannot be restored by Repetition. Segment health is sampled at fixed world spacing, not pointer-event density.

## Review focus

Resource generation through Collection/Repetition; frame-rate-dependent damage; orientation mistakes in Viseur; unbreakable rings caused by sampling; repairs replenishing broken segments for free; invisible particles still colliding; unsupported catalogue entries pretending to work; mobile HUD covering the arena; AI bypassing player constraints.
